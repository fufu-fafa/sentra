Directions you can feel

SENTRA is a navigation sleeve for blind children. It guides them along a recorded route with gentle vibration, warns of obstacles ahead, and lets parents follow the trip.

One app, two phones

The child's phone

It connects to the sleeve over Bluetooth and does the navigating.

  • Records a route once, then guides the child along it
  • Speaks each turn aloud while the sleeve vibrates
  • Notices when the child goes off route

The parent's phone: SENTRA Care

It shows how the trip is going, in the app or in a web browser.

  • Live location on a map and a history of trips
  • Alarms if the child goes off route or the sleeve disconnects
  • An alarm if the child's phone goes silent during a trip

Guidance continues without internet

The sleeve and navigation don't depend on a connection. While the child's phone is offline, trip updates wait on it and are sent when it can reach SENTRA Care again. Parents don't see live updates during that time.

SENTRA is a prototype in development. It doesn't replace a white cane, mobility training or adult supervision.
